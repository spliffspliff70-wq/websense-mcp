fix(diff): SVG drawing attributes are rendering, not structure

Found by living with the tool rather than reading it: after Ali asked me to confirm the
DIFF was wired, I scrolled the x.com tab and read the auto-DIFF back.

    DIFF (auto, after scroll): ... "structure":{"changed":[...89 entries...],"count":89}
    ... "mutated":true

89 "structure" changes from a SCROLL. The viewport group did its job (the 2,000 moved
elements collapsed to one line, "Not a mutation"), but the bulk of those 89 were icons
redrawing their path data plus points/transform on other shapes — rendering, arriving
under the heading that is supposed to mean the page really changed. Scroll churn as a
mutation is the exact bug this design exists to prevent.

isPresentationAttr already covered class/style/dir/lang/data-*; it did not cover the SVG
spec's drawing attributes. Added: d, points, transform, viewBox, fill, stroke,
stroke-*, fill/stroke-opacity, opacity, cx, cy, r, rx, ry, x1/y1/x2/y2, offset,
stop-color, stop-opacity, gradientUnits, preserveAspectRatio. These are standards-level
drawing attributes, not a site vocabulary, and a change to one is a repaint in the same
way a style change is. role/id/value/disabled/checked/href/aria-* stay structural.

Tested as BEHAVIOUR, not by grepping source: the predicate is extracted out of the
collector template literal and executed, asserting 17 rendering attrs classify true and
12 structural attrs classify false — so nothing structural can be swept in later.

ALSO: tools/precheck-backticks.mjs, wired in front of the suite (`npm test`).
The in-suite backtick guard is useless on its own — test-regressions.mjs imports the
collectors, so a stray backtick makes the module fail to PARSE and the suite dies before
the guard can report anything. I proved that the hard way today: I wrote the SVG comment
with backticks inside it, broke the module, and spent a cycle on a SyntaxError whose cause
was a comment I had just written. The new check runs first, scans only the template BODY
between the delimiters (so legitimate backticks elsewhere in a file are not flagged), and
was verified against an injected fault.

154 tests pass.
