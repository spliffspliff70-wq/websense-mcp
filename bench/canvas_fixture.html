canvas coordinate-click fixture

Two canvases (2D and WebGL). Each records the coordinates it RECEIVES on click — the browser-computed offsetX/offsetY, the clientX/clientY the event carried, and the local point derived from clientX minus the canvas rect. Nothing here is inferred from a tool reply: the point of this page is that the PAGE writes down what it got, and a checker reads it back.
