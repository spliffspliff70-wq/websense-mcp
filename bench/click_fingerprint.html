click fingerprint fixture

Attaches a capture-phase listener for every mouse/pointer/focus event and records the FULL property set of each one, plus the side state a site can read at that moment. The question this answers is not "did the click work" but "what exactly does the page see", so a synthetic click can be compared field by field against a genuinely trusted one.
